Mountains whisper first, PRAVAH listens first.

Built for the most vulnerable slopes of North-East India, PRAVAH fuses geotechnical pressure, live rainfall, and soil saturation data to raise the alarm before the mountain ever falls.

01 / HYDRO-DYNAMICS

Antecedent Rain Influx

Every terrain voxel across the Western Ghats and Himalayan belts is scored continuously against cumulative 72-hour precipitation and saturation thresholds.

02 / VOLUMETRIC DEM

3D Terrain Draping

Switch seamlessly from planar 2D overview maps into an immersive 62° tilted 3D elevation mesh draped with live satellite and shear scarp contours.

03 / POINT INSPECTION

Localized Scarp Telemetry

Select any auto-discovered red beacon across Indian mountain corridors to fly the 3D camera directly into the slope and inspect its Factor of Safety ($FoS$).

Continuous Dissemination Protocol

Engineered for Instant Disaster Response across India.

Common Alerting Protocol (CAP) emergency broadcasts dispatch directly to State and District Disaster Management Authorities the exact moment slope stability indices breach safety thresholds.
